# Define Stock Availability, Holds, and Reservations

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01, 02, 03
Labels: `wayfinder:grilling`

## Question

How should the system calculate usable stock at each location? Decide how on-hand, damaged, in-transit, picked, held, and reserved quantities affect availability, and whether orders or other work can reserve stock before dispatch or use.
