# 11: Transfer Stock Between Locations

**What to build:** Staff initiate, receive, and trace a transfer so source, destination, and in-transit or available quantities stay consistent throughout the agreed transfer workflow.

**Blocked by:** 09: Receive Stock and Find It by Location; 04: Define Stock Availability, Holds, and Reservations; 06: Define Staff Roles, Permissions, and Audit Controls.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] An authorized user can initiate a transfer of an eligible quantity from an allowed source location to an allowed destination.
- [ ] The transfer status and quantities at source, in transit, and destination follow the agreed inventory rules.
- [ ] An authorized user can complete or resolve a transfer, with each stock change attributed and traceable.
- [ ] The end-to-end acceptance test verifies the transfer lifecycle and resulting persisted quantities through the user-facing application.
