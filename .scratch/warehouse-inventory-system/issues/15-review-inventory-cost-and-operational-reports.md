# 15: Review Inventory Cost and Operational Reports

**What to build:** Managers inspect the agreed inventory cost or valuation and operational reports based on traceable stock and purchasing activity.

**Blocked by:** 08: Define Cost Valuation and Inventory Reporting; 09: Receive Stock and Find It by Location; and any relevant shipped workflow among 10: Count Stock and Resolve Discrepancies, 11: Transfer Stock Between Locations, 12: Pick and Dispatch Stock, and 13: Work Offline and Safely Synchronize Inventory Changes.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] An authorized manager can view the inventory values and operational measures selected by the reporting decision.
- [ ] Report totals and filters follow the agreed valuation rules, location scope, and time basis.
- [ ] Reported values can be traced to the underlying inventory activity at the level agreed for the product.
- [ ] The end-to-end acceptance test verifies report results against known, persisted inventory workflows through the user-facing application.
