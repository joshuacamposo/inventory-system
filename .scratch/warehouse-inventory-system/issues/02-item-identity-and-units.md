# Define Item Identity, Barcodes, and Stocking Units

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 01
Labels: `wayfinder:grilling`

## Question

What identifies a stock item in this warehouse? Decide how products, variants/SKUs, supplier item codes, barcodes, package sizes, and unit conversions relate, and when stock must be distinguished by lot, serial number, or expiry date. Specify the minimum item data and uniqueness rules the product spec needs.
